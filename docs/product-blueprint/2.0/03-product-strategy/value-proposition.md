# Value Proposition

**For** a person who wants to remember and celebrate important relationships, **AI-Birthday** is a private local-first birthday assistant that provides one coherent remember→prepare→review→handoff workflow. Unlike generic calendars or ungrounded AI chat, it carries recipient context, annual occurrence logic and explicit delivery evidence. (`ER`; user-demand strength is `UA`.)

## Outcome hypotheses

- Fewer missed/last-minute birthday actions (`PC`; requires baseline measurement).
- Less drafting effort while retaining personal control (`PC`; requires task-completion evidence).
- Higher trust through grounded facts and truthful history (`ER`).
- Recoverability across app failure/device/account change (`ER`).

No market-share, conversion, revenue or retention target is asserted.
