# Vision, Mission and Principles

**Vision — ER/EPD, high confidence:** Make celebrating important people dependable, personal and low-friction without surrendering control of their data or message.

**Mission:** Convert trusted birthday facts into timely, reviewable and recoverable actions.

## Principles

1. User control before automation (`EPD`).
2. AI drafts; it does not silently send (`EPD`).
3. AI must not invent recipient facts (`EPD`).
4. Manual/offline value survives AI/provider/cloud failure (`EPD`).
5. Entitlement, provider capability, delivery channel and delivery evidence are separate (`EPD/ER`).
6. Handoff is not send; user confirmation is not external-delivery proof (`EPD`).
7. Privacy, accessibility, observability and recoverability are feature completion criteria (`ER`).
8. One canonical owner per invariant/aggregate; infrastructure cannot invent product state (`ER`).
9. Production claims require named environment evidence (`ER`).
