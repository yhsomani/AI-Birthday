# Product Discovery

## Problem

Important relationships are time-sensitive, but birthdays are scattered across devices and generic reminders stop before a meaningful action. AI drafting can help, but generic output, fabricated facts, loss of privacy or ambiguous “sent” state can erode trust. (`ER`, supported by existing product decisions and architecture; customer-demand intensity remains `UA`.)

## Jobs to be done

- When an important birthday approaches, help me know exactly whom to contact and when.
- Keep recipient facts private and accurate without managing a CRM.
- Produce a useful message quickly, while retaining editorial and send control.
- Recover from missed reminders, AI failure, app closure and device/account change without losing trust.
- Understand what was handed off, confirmed, failed or never acted on.

## Strategic boundaries

The product is a personal assistant, not a social network, CRM, campaign sender, autonomous WhatsApp system, analytics platform, AI chat product, calendar replacement or cloud-first contact manager. (`EPD/ER`; high confidence.)

## Opportunity and validation

`PC` adjacent opportunities include richer import review, guided accessibility, privacy-safe analytics and separate Autopilot. None may enter core until user need, consent, security, reliability, commercial and operational cases are approved. Market size, demand, price and competitors remain `UA`; interview/survey/conversion evidence must precede claims.
