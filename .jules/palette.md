## 2024-05-18 - Typographic Scale & Rhythm UI Redesign
**Learning:** Generic Material theme variants and unscaled typography make consumer apps feel sterile and identical to B2B dashboards. Relying on raw `Colors.grey.withOpacity(0.1)` fragments a design system and fails accessibility checks.
**Action:** Always anchor all text styling to `Theme.of(context).textTheme` with specific `GoogleFonts` definitions in `AppTheme`, and leverage `colorScheme.surfaceContainerHighest` and `withValues(alpha:)` rather than hardcoded hex opacities. Wrap scrolling lists in `MediaQuery` padding to enforce max-width constraints on wide viewports (tablet/web).
