Privacy control

Delete your WishWell account

This starts permanent deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and associated WishWell server records. You do not need to reinstall the mobile app.

Read before continuing

  • The server first blocks new Android automation permits. A previously issued permit may still submit before its frozen deadline, normally no more than one minute.
  • The server then recursively removes app-operated coordination records and verifies their absence before deleting the Firebase account.
  • This website cannot erase protected data that remains on a phone. Use the app’s local wipe control on each device, or remove the app, and remember that uninstalling alone does not delete the online account.
  • Deletion does not remove source contacts from Google, messages in Android’s SMS Provider, Apple Messages or iCloud copies, carrier records, recipients’ copies, or backups outside the app’s control.
  • Deleting the Firebase account does not automatically promise revocation of every Google permission granted to this app. You can separately remove WishWell access in your Google Account.

If you cannot access Google

Do not send identity documents to an unlisted address. Use only the identity-verified support workflow linked below. Verified administrative requests use the same Android permit drain and recursive deletion process; there is no unsafe shortcut.

An authenticated request is acknowledged on this page immediately. Verified support requests are acknowledged within 7 calendar days. Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days unless a disclosed legal hold applies. A request receipt confirms acceptance, not completion.
