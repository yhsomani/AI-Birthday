Your birthday plan stays private by design

This notice explains how Birthday Autopilot handles information in the Android Automation Edition, the iOS Companion Edition, and this public website.

What stays on your device

Names, birthdays, phone numbers, selected recipients, approved messages, schedules, proposals, and ordinary activity are kept in app-owned protected storage on each device. They are not copied into an app-operated contact cloud. Android and iPhone authorize and store their own working set; raw contact data is not synchronized between devices.

Google account and Contacts

The app uses the Google account you choose for Firebase identity and asks separately for read-only Google Contacts access. It reads only names, birthdays, phone numbers, and contact metadata needed for sync. Source contacts remain in your Google account. Disconnecting Contacts removes app-derived local data but does not revoke Google access; revoking access is a separate action and can affect every Google scope granted to this app.

Google, Firebase, Android, and Apple SDKs manage credentials internally. Birthday Autopilot does not put access tokens, refresh tokens, passwords, or provider credentials in React Native JavaScript, app logs, app databases, diagnostics, or this website’s visible interface.

Minimal server data

Firebase Authentication holds the app account identity. Firebase Authentication, Installations, App Check, and Remote Config also process account, installation, app/device-integrity, project, request, security, and operational-flag metadata. Remote Config uses a Firebase Installations token to fetch the bounded AI feature flag. This metadata is content-free, not data-free: it does not include raw Contacts or birthday-message content, but it crosses the device boundary.

The server-only coordination service keeps content-free account mode, active Android installation and sender epoch, short authorization timing, usage budgets, and keyed opaque birthday or destination guards. Only for people enabled on Android, the mobile app sends fixed-length pseudonymous occurrence and destination bases plus bounded timing and sender-fence values; the server immediately transforms the bases with a secret HMAC. These records can be linked within the protected account for duplicate prevention and are not anonymous. iOS does not register recipients with this service and cannot send automatically, but it can make content-free account-status and cleanup requests for safe Android coexistence. Firestore never stores raw contacts, People IDs, birthdays, phone numbers, message text, prompts, or approval content, and mobile clients have no direct Firestore access.

Gemini assistance

Gemini is optional. A suggestion request contains only the selected language, tone, name style, and SMS-part limit in a generic template-writing prompt. Contact names, birthdays, phone numbers, relationships, message history, and the current saved or draft message are not sent to Gemini. AI monitoring of prompts and responses is disabled for the production configuration.

Messages leave the app

On supported Android devices, an approved SMS is disclosed to Android’s telephony stack, the system SMS Provider, the carrier, and the recipient. On iPhone, the proposed recipient and draft are passed to Apple’s editable MessageUI composer; the user can change them and controls Send or Cancel. Apple controls the available sender line and final SMS, MMS, or iMessage transport. These system, carrier, recipient, Messages, iCloud, and backup copies are outside Birthday Autopilot’s control and are not erased by app or account deletion.

Retention

  • Ordinary local activity, diagnostic detail, retired callback identities, and terminal test detail are kept for no more than 30 days.
  • Content-free birthday duplicate-safety guards may remain for 400 days while Contacts stay connected, so a deleted and recreated contact cannot receive a duplicate annual greeting.
  • Never-used server authorization expires after 10 minutes and becomes cleanup-eligible after 24 hours. Test records are kept for 30 days. Inactive or revoked Android installation records are kept for no more than 90 or 30 days respectively.
  • External deletion identity and support evidence is removed within 30 days after resolution. A content-free request time and outcome receipt may remain for one year for compliance evidence.

These product retention periods do not set every provider’s retention. Firebase, Google, Gemini, carrier, Messages, iCloud, and backup providers may retain authentication, installation, attestation, request/response, operational, abuse-prevention, billing, or transport records under their own policies and legal duties. App or account deletion handles Birthday Autopilot-controlled data; it cannot promise immediate erasure of provider logs or outside copies.

This website

The public site has no advertising, analytics, session replay, or non-essential cookies. The deletion page uses Firebase Auth and reCAPTCHA Enterprise App Check only when preparing the secure sign-in workflow. Firebase Auth persistence is memory-only for this page. It does not request Contacts scope or read a Google access token. To recover an uncertain submission after reload, it keeps only the random pending deletion receipt in this tab’s temporary session storage until exact completion is displayed or you clear it. The receipt is not put in a URL, local storage, cookie, analytics event, or log. Google and Firebase may process network and security metadata under their terms.

Your controls

The app provides separate controls to pause, disconnect Contacts, revoke Google access, sign out with retained or wiped setup, clear templates or activity, wipe app-owned local data, and delete the app account. The public deletion page starts Firebase-account and associated server-data deletion without reinstalling the app. If you cannot access the connected Google account, use the identity-verified support workflow.
