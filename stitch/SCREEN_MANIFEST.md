## Final UI principles

1. One calm, neutral, trustworthy, sparse system across both editions: Material 3 behavior on Android and familiar iOS navigation, sheets, controls, and spacing. No confetti, cake clutter, script fonts, streaks, or gamified sending.
2. Three permanent tabs only: **Home**, **People**, and **Settings**. Activity, setup, approval, transfer, repair, and deletion are task flows.
3. One primary action per screen. Minimum 48dp targets, system typography, clear hierarchy, and layouts that remain usable at 200% text.
4. Every status uses icon plus text. Never rely on color, motion, or an optimistic animation to communicate safety or delivery.
5. Use plain dates such as `Tue, 14 July`. Mask phone numbers in lists; show a full number only in an explicit approval or Test context.
6. Use initials, not contact photos. The app does not request contact photos.
7. Permissions are requested in context, never as a setup wall. System-owned Google, Android, iOS, Settings, MessageUI, composer, and share surfaces are not imitated.
8. After a system handoff, show **Checking???**, re-read the real state, and then show a verified pass/fail result. Opening Settings is not proof that a condition changed.
9. All contacts start Off. Gemini never selects, ranks, or infers recipients and never receives contact PII.
10. Message preview is deterministic. Android displays placeholder validation, Unicode character/segment count, SIM context, and cost warning before immutable approval. iOS displays the proposed recipient/draft, reminder context, possible charges, and the fact that MessageUI remains editable while iOS/Messages controls any available sender-line and SMS/MMS/iMessage choice; the app can neither choose nor observe the final values.
11. Truthful outcome copy is mandatory. Android Submitted is **Sending from this phone** and sent callbacks permit **Sent from this phone; delivery not confirmed**. iOS uses only **Composer opened**, **Composer cancelled**, **Composer failed**, **Composer outcome unknown**, and **Messages reported sent; delivery not confirmed**. Missing evidence is never called failure or delivery.
12. Destructive actions name exactly what is deleted, what remains, any remote-pending state, and the fact that Android SMS Provider/carrier/recipient and iOS Messages/iCloud/carrier/recipient copies are outside app control.
13. English and Hindi are the MVP visual locales. Other locales fall back to English. Pseudo-RTL is a layout test, not a supported-language claim.
14. Light, dark, high-contrast-aware, reduced-motion, TalkBack, VoiceOver, switch/keyboard, Dynamic Type/200% text, and bidi-safe behavior are part of every component definition.

## Cross-screen state and accessibility contract

Every component and route must define:

- loading/skeleton, empty, offline, stale, disabled, focus, pressed, error, retry, in-progress, remote-pending, complete, and partial/failure states as applicable;
- Android Automation on/Paused and iOS Reminders on/Paused, plus Needs your attention, Limited, Unsupported, notification-limited, horizon-partial, MessageUI-unavailable, and safety-status-unavailable readiness states;
- signed in, retained signed out, reconnect required, Android another-active-sender/transfer pending, iOS companion-only/Managed by Android, and deleting account states;
- Enabled, Ready/Off, Needs attention, Unavailable, Excluded, source deleted, and approval invalidated contact states;
- light, dark, high-contrast-aware, reduced-motion, 200% text/Dynamic Type, English, Hindi-layout-fixture, and pseudo-RTL/bidi-safe variants;
- TalkBack and VoiceOver label, role, state, hint, logical focus order, rotor/grouping sanity, and live error/status announcement as applicable;
- switch/keyboard access and no swipe-only action;
- minimum 48dp/pt targets and no clipped, ellipsized critical, or horizontally scrolling consent/status copy;
- one primary action, persistent critical errors, and exact destructive consequences;
- generic lock-screen notification copy with no name, number, birthday, or message; and
- no real PII, credentials, reversible IDs, or diagnostic content in examples.

Outcome copy must preserve these distinctions:

- **Submitted:** `Sending from this phone` ??? Android API acceptance is not carrier acceptance.
- **Sent from device:** `Sent from this phone; delivery not confirmed.`
- **Delivered:** only when every expected delivery callback succeeds.
- **Delivery not confirmed / Partial delivery unknown / Submission unknown:** never relabel as failure or authorize a blind retry.
- **Safety check unavailable:** `Safety check unavailable; no text was sent.`
- **iOS Composer opened:** MessageUI is system-owned and still requires the user to review and tap Send.
- **iOS Composer cancelled / failed:** exact result only; a new app action requires a fresh foreground review.
- **iOS Composer reported sent:** `Messages reported sent; delivery not confirmed.` The app does not know the final edited payload or sender line.
- **iOS Composer outcome unknown:** never relabel as Sent/Failed and never offer a second in-app composer for that occurrence.
